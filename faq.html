Frequently asked questions

If your question isn’t here, just ask.

What should I wear?

Anything comfortable. You stay fully clothed and only take off your shoes. Soft, loose layers are ideal. You might want to skip a belt or bulky jewelry.

Do I have to believe in Reiki for it to work?

No. You don’t need any particular beliefs, spiritual or otherwise. Plenty of people come skeptical or just curious. All you need to do is lie down and rest.

What happens during a session?

We start with a short conversation. Then you lie face up on a padded table with a pillow and blanket while Caroline rests her hands lightly on, or just above, a series of places from head to feet. It’s quiet and unhurried. Afterward there’s time to sit up slowly and ask questions. More detail on the About Reiki page.

Will I be touched? Can I ask for no touch?

Touch is always light and non-invasive, never massage, and never anywhere private. If you’d prefer no touch at all, Caroline will keep her hands a few inches above your body. Just say so at any point, before or during the session.

What will I feel?

It varies. Many people feel deeply relaxed, notice warmth or tingling, or fall asleep. Some don’t notice much during the session but feel calmer afterward. There’s no wrong experience.

Can I come if I’m going through medical treatment?

Yes. Many people find Reiki a restful complement to treatment. It doesn’t replace medical care, and Caroline doesn’t give medical advice. It’s a good idea to let your care team know. Please mention anything relevant (like a port, recent surgery, or trouble lying flat) when you book so the session can be set up comfortably for you.

Is Reiki the same as massage?

No. There’s no pressure, rubbing or manipulation of muscles, and you stay clothed. Hands simply rest still for a few minutes in each position.
