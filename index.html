Who comes to see me

People arrive for all kinds of reasons.

Most are simply looking for a calm, unhurried hour where someone is taking care of them. You don’t need a reason beyond that.

  • Stress and burnout

    A full stop in a week that hasn’t had one.

  • Chronic illness

    Gentle support alongside the care you already receive.

  • Grief and loss

    Space to be still, without having to talk if you’d rather not.

  • Athletes

    A restful counterweight to hard training and recovery.

  • During medical treatment

    A quiet, comfortable hour between appointments.

  • Simply curious

    Wondering what Reiki is like is reason enough to try it.

What a session is like

Clothed, comfortable, and at your pace.

Read more about Reiki

  1. We talk for a few minutes

    About how you’re feeling and anything you’d like me to know. You share only what you want to.

  2. You lie down, fully clothed

    On a padded table with pillows and a blanket. You only take off your shoes.

  3. I place my hands lightly, or just above

    Resting gently on the head, shoulders, torso and legs, or held a few inches away if you prefer no touch. Many people drift off.

  4. You take a few minutes to come back

    A glass of water, a moment to sit up slowly, and time for any questions.
